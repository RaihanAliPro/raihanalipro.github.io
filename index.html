Raihan Ali is an EMC & Product Safety Engineer at Keysight Technologies in Böblingen, Germany (since July 2017). With over 7 years of specialized laboratory experience, he leads and executes electromagnetic compatibility compliance testing for advanced medical equipment and electronic instruments.

His core focus includes IEC 60601-1-2 (EMC for medical electrical devices and systems), ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratory quality systems, EN 61000-3-2 (harmonic emissions), power factor correction criteria, and general electrical product safety verifications.

Raihan has deep proficiency in RadiMation® EMC test automation software, spectrum analyzers, EMI receivers, and RF test chambers. For hardware design and simulation, he uses Altium Designer, EAGLE, LTspice, MATLAB Simulink, and Proteus.

He earned a Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Electrical Engineering from BTU Cottbus-Senftenberg, Germany (Master thesis on Boost Converters with Smart PFC conforming to EN 61000-3-2), and a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Electrical Engineering from UET Lahore, Pakistan.
